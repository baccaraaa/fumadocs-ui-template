## System Specifications

For optimal performance, we recommend:

<ul className="list-disc list-inside my-4">
  <li><strong>Operating System:</strong> Windows 10 or Windows 11</li>
  <li><strong>Processor:</strong> Multi-core processor (4+ cores recommended)</li>
  <li><strong>Memory:</strong> 8GB RAM minimum, 16GB+ recommended</li>
  <li><strong>Storage:</strong> SSD storage with at least 256GB free space</li>
  <li><strong>Network:</strong> Stable internet connection</li>
</ul>

<Callout type="warning">
  ⚠️ Older operating systems or hardware configurations may experience reduced performance or compatibility issues with certain 20rank features.
</Callout>
